# Athletic Intelligence: Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**KU x Techstars Startup Weekend 2026 · Companion to `TEAM_BRIEF.md`**
**Source:** founder interview with Xavier Hiemstra, Saturday Nov 14, plus the team brief
**Status:** living document. Marked **[VALIDATED]**, **[FOUNDER VIEW]** (Xavier's informed judgment, not yet tested on strangers), or **[HYPOTHESIS]** (needs Saturday interviews). Fix it in this repo if it's wrong.

> **Read this first:** an ICP is not "everyone who might use the app." It is the *one* type of person who feels the problem most sharply, is easiest for us to reach this weekend, and is most likely to pay. We build the demo, the pitch, and Saturday's interviews around this person. Everyone else is a later expansion.

---

## 1. The ICP in one sentence

**A tech-forward endurance hobbyist who has done a few races, trains with real health data from their wearables, but can't turn that data into a smart plan on their own — and wants coach-quality training without a coach's price.**

The sharpest version of this person right now is a **hobbyist triathlete.** See §4 for why the target shifted from marathoners.

## 3. What drives them (jobs, pains, triggers)

**The job they're hiring us for:** *"Turn everything my body and my devices already know into a plan that actually gets me to my goal — without paying for a human coach."*

**The core pain — data overload. [VALIDATED by founder's own experience]**
They have the numbers. Heart rate, sleep, VO2, recovery, pace, swim/bike/run splits — all of it is being tracked. But it lives in separate apps and none of it feeds their actual training plan. The data is a pile, not a plan.

**The trigger that sends them looking:** the moment they realize they have mountains of data and no idea what to *do* with it. (Not a plateau, not an injury — the frustration of unused information.)

**The outcome they want:** **professional-level training for a fraction of the cost.** This is the promise the whole product hangs on and the line the ICP responds to.

## 4. Why triathletes, not marathoners (the target shift)

The brief launched with marathon/distance running as the wedge. Based on Saturday's thinking, **hobbyist triathletes are now the primary target**, with marathoners moved to secondary.

**Reasons [FOUNDER VIEW]:**
1. **Harder to plan** — balancing three disciplines is exactly the planning problem our AI solves; a marathoner only has to plan running.
2. **Higher willingness to pay** — triathletes already spend heavily on gear, races and coaching, so $20/mo is a small line item.
3. **More data, more scattered** — swim, bike and run generate data across more devices and apps, so the "one hub" value is bigger.
4. **Personal experience** — Xavier is starting triathlon training and feels this problem firsthand.

**Evidence level: [HYPOTHESIS] — a few conversations only.** This is the single most important thing to validate Saturday. If triathletes don't confirm it, marathoners are the fallback primary.

## 6. Current alternatives (what they use instead of us)

The ICP's workaround today is some mix of:
1. **Checking each app separately** — flipping between Garmin, Oura, Strava, swim app, etc., and mentally stitching it together.
2. **Pasting their data into ChatGPT or Claude** — which gives decent advice but has no persistent view of their data and no memory of how past workouts felt.

Both are friction and guesswork. Our wedge is that we hold *all* the data *and* the history of how each workout landed, so the plan compounds over time. A fresh chatbot prompt starts from zero every time.

---

## 7. Who is NOT the ICP (anti-profile)

Saying no is what makes the ICP useful. We explicitly do **not** target, for now:

- **Elite / professional athletes** — already have coaches, staff and custom tooling. Not our fight.
- **Non-trackers** — anyone with no wearable or health data. We have nothing to work with; they're not ready for us.
- **Total beginners who've never raced** — don't yet feel the planning pain or generate enough data.
- **Pure marathoners / runners** — still a real market, but **secondary** this weekend. Expansion segment, not launch focus.
- **Other sports** (bodybuilding, general fitness) — on the long-term roadmap, not the ICP.

## 8. One-paragraph persona (for the pitch)

> *Meet our athlete: she's 29, works full-time, and signed up for her first Olympic-distance triathlon. She wears a watch and a recovery ring and has months of data she doesn't know how to use. She can't afford a $200/month tri coach and doesn't have time to build her own three-sport plan, so she's been pasting numbers into ChatGPT and hoping. She'd happily pay around $20 a month for something that finally connects all her data and tells her exactly what to do — and lets her book a real coach for a check-in when she's stuck.*

*(Swap gender/age freely — the ICP is behavior-defined. Use one concrete persona on stage; judges remember a person, not a demographic.)*
